Privacy Policy

Stub - last updated September 5, 2026. Not legal advice.

This policy describes how CI Intelligence handles information in the current build.

Data we store

  • Account data: email, password hash, organization name, API tokens.
  • CI analysis data: workflow run payloads you ingest (jobs, steps, test results, messages).

Storage

Local / self-hosted deployments persist JSON under the configured DATA_DIR (for example data/store.json and data/accounts.json). Production deployments should use encrypted storage, access controls, and retention policies.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The demo Bearer token demo is for local evaluation only and must not be used in production.

Your choices

Contact the operator of your deployment to request account deletion or export. Replace this stub with a counsel-reviewed privacy policy before processing customer PII in production.
